Paws & Homes was founded on a simple belief: every animal deserves a loving, safe, and permanent home. We are dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming pets in need.

Our Mission

We believe that compassion changes lives. Since our founding, Paws & Homes has worked tirelessly to pull vulnerable animals from high-kill shelters and dangerous situations on the streets.

Our dedicated team of volunteers and veterinarians provide immediate medical care, behavioral rehabilitation, and a safe haven until they are ready to meet their forever families.

The Matchmaking Process

We don't just find homes; we find the right homes. Our adoption process is carefully designed to ensure compatibility between the pet's personality and the adopter's lifestyle.

By taking the time to understand the unique needs of every animal and human who walks through our doors, we boast a 98% permanent placement success rate.

What We Stand For

Compassion

We treat every animal with the kindness, dignity, and respect they deserve, regardless of their past or medical condition.

Integrity

We maintain full transparency with our adopters, providing honest assessments of an animal's health, temperament, and history.

Community

We believe animal welfare is a collective effort. We actively educate and support our community to foster responsible pet ownership.
